PowerFreeze customer voice

Phase 3 customer survey, Nigeria. 612 responses collected 28 September to 7 October 2026.

Filter the survey

How to read this: scores are on a 1 to 10 scale. The NPS-style score is the share of 9 and 10 answers minus the share of 6 and below, shown in points. Themes for written answers were grouped by keyword rules and are approximate. Thirty-three account numbers appear more than once, so a few customers were interviewed twice; removing them changes the headline figures by under one percentage point. Areas with few responses are directional only. No names, phone numbers or emails are included on this page.

Service technicians: PowerFreeze after-sales

Eleven technicians, one per region, surveyed 23 to 26 September 2026.

Filter the survey

How to read this: with 11 responses, read counts rather than percentages. Escalations per day of "More than 5" is counted as 5 in the total, so the headline is a floor. The share of resolutions from customer education was cleaned because some technicians answered as a fraction (0.8) and others as a percentage (80). Written recommendations were coded into themes by reading each answer. Quotes are unattributed and no emails are included.
